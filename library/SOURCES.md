# Practice library sources

Built by scripts/build-library.mjs. Questions are reused under their licences; Mnemoq adds the sets, choices for numeric answers, and review cards.

- **Vocabulary builder**: Princeton WordNet, WordNet licence, https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
- **Maths word problems**: GSM8K (OpenAI), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k
- **Quantitative reasoning**: AQuA-RAT (DeepMind), Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/datasets/deepmind/aqua_rat
- **School mathematics**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Higher mathematics**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Physics**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Chemistry**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Biology**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Computer science**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Economics**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu
- **Geography**: MMLU (Hendrycks et al.), MIT, https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu

MMLU: Hendrycks et al., "Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding" (ICLR 2021), MIT licence.
AQuA-RAT: Ling et al., "Program Induction by Rationale Generation" (ACL 2017), Apache License 2.0.
GSM8K: Cobbe et al., "Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems" (2021), MIT licence.
