# Mnemoq

### Remember better. Learn smarter.

**Turn your mistakes into knowledge that sticks.**

Mnemoq is a personal learning workspace that transforms mistakes and important facts into interactive review cards. Test yourself with multiple-choice questions or practise recalling answers from memory, then revisit difficult material through scheduled reviews.

[**Try Mnemoq →**](https://mnemoq-two.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Multiple-Choice Review Cards

**Test your knowledge, recognize the right answer, and learn from every mistake.**

Mnemoq turns your saved learning cards into multiple-choice review questions, letting you practise recognition and reinforce what you've learned.

- **Automatic answer choices** generated from your existing learning library.
- **Interactive quizzes** that let you select an answer instead of simply rereading a card.
- **Instant feedback** to help you identify correct and incorrect answers.
- **Targeted revision** using cards from your selected subject.
- **Flexible practice** with the ability to switch between multiple-choice and typed-answer modes.

Multiple-choice review makes revision more engaging while helping you identify gaps in your understanding. For stronger recall, you can switch to typed-answer mode and retrieve the answer without seeing the options.

### 🧠 Active Recall

Go beyond recognizing answers and practise retrieving information from memory.

- Type your answer before revealing the solution.
- Compare your response with the correct answer.
- Reveal answers when you get stuck.
- Revisit the original answer you gave incorrectly.
- Switch review modes according to your needs.

### ⚡ Quick Add

Capture learning material with minimal friction.

- Choose a subject and category.
- Save multiple cards consecutively.
- Navigate fields using your keyboard.
- Require only the question and correct answer.
- Optionally add your original answer and a memory hook.
- Review a summary when you've finished adding cards.

### 🔁 Scheduled Reviews

Rate how well you remembered each answer to schedule future reviews.

| Rating | Review behavior |
|---|---|
| Again | Review again in about 10 minutes |
| Hard | Repeat the previous interval, with a 1-day minimum for a new card |
| Good | Double the previous interval |
| Easy | Triple the interval, with a minimum of 3 days |

Cards return when they're due, helping you focus on material that needs more attention.

### 📚 Personal Learning Library

- Organize cards by subject and category.
- Create custom subjects and categories.
- Filter and browse your collection.
- Edit or delete cards.
- Start reviewing a card directly from the library.

### 📊 Progress Dashboard

Track total cards, cards due for review, cards still to review, and mastered items.

### ☁️ Cloud-Backed Accounts

Sign up and log in with Supabase to keep your learning data associated with your account.
